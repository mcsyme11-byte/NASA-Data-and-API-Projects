Section 8

Writing

List

  • Close approach: the moment an object passes nearest to Earth.
  • LD: lunar distance, about 384,400 km.

Glossary

Sol
A day on Mars, 24 hours 39 minutes.
Ls
Solar longitude: where Mars is in its year, from 0° to 360°.

Steps

  1. Pick a week. Use Prev and Next.
  2. Filter. Choose what to show.

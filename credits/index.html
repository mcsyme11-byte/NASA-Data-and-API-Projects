ESA (the European Space Agency)

ESA data and imagery are added alongside NASA's where they help. Each ESA source is listed here, with its exact credit line, from the day it appears on the site; ESA's credit is also shown wherever its data or pictures appear. Some ESA material is released under the CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO licence (credit in ESA's own form, for example "ESA/DLR/FU Berlin, CC BY-SA 3.0 IGO"); other ESA material follows ESA's terms and conditions. ESA's logo is not used.

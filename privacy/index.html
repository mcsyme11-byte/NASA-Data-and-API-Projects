About this site

Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

NASA and ESA Data Projects collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do on the site is sent to the developer.

What is kept in your browser

A few things are saved in your own browser, on your own device, to make the site work. They are never sent to the developer.

  • No key. The site does not ask for or keep a NASA API key. A key saved by an earlier version of the Asteroid Tracker is deleted from your browser the next time you open it.
  • Where you were, such as the open tab, your filters and your scroll position, so the "Back" buttons return you to the same place. This is kept in your browser's session storage and is deleted when you close the tab.
  • The place you choose in the Asteroid Tracker's "Visible from here" tab or in Sky Finder (a town's name and its latitude and longitude, or your device's location rounded to about 1 km), so it is there next time. It is kept in your browser's local storage and used only on your device to work out what is in your sky; "Forget this place" deletes it.
  • Sky Finder's settings: that you have read its safety notice, whether red light is on, the camera view width, and its compass calibration (two angles, kept for 12 hours). Local storage, on your device only.
  • Sky Finder's offline copy: Sky Finder saves its own pages and data files in your browser's cache (a service worker) so it still works with a weak connection outdoors. Nothing about you is in it; your browser's "clear site data" removes it.

Sky Finder: camera, location and motion sensors

Sky Finder asks for three permissions, each only when you use the feature that needs it, and you can refuse any of them:

  • Camera: the picture is shown on your screen behind the markers and nothing else. It is never recorded, saved, analysed or sent anywhere. The camera is turned off when you leave the camera view.
  • Location: used on your device to work out which way to look. It is never sent anywhere; the data files Sky Finder reads are the same for every visitor. You can type a town instead.
  • Motion and orientation: used on your device, while the camera view is open, to know where the phone points. Readings are not kept or sent.

Browsers show their own prompt for each permission, and you can withdraw it at any time in the browser's site settings.

Services your browser contacts

The pages load data and code straight from the services below. Like any website, each one receives the basic technical information every browser sends (your IP address, browser type and the address requested), under its own privacy policy.

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site. GitHub logs each visitor's IP address for security (GitHub Pages data collection, GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • Pages that read only this site's own files: the spacecraft map, the "Visible from here" tab and Sky Finder contact no other service; their data (from NASA/JPL Horizons and CelesTrak) is copied to the site twice a day.
  • NASA and NASA/JPL services that supply the data and imagery: api.nasa.gov, GIBS, the NASA Image and Video Library, EONET, the Gateway to Astronaut Photography of Earth (eol.jsc.nasa.gov, whose pictures the Image Explorer's globe shows) and JPL's Solar System Dynamics (NASA Web Privacy Policy).
  • The US Geological Survey (earthquake.usgs.gov): the Image Explorer's globe reads the past week's earthquakes from it (USGS privacy policies).
  • ESA/Webb and ESA/Hubble (esawebb.org, esahubble.org and their picture servers cdn.esawebb.org and cdn.esahubble.org): the Image Explorer's Picture library and globe search their picture archives and show their pictures, under those sites' own privacy policies (ESA/Hubble privacy policy; ESA/Webb's is linked from its site).

Links to other sites

Some pages link to NASA, ESA (including its NEO Coordination Centre), the Minor Planet Center, other space agencies' mission pages, CelesTrak and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If the site ever starts storing or sharing anything new, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.

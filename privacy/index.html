About this site

Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

NASA and ESA Data Projects collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you do on the site is sent to the developer.

What is kept in your browser

A few things are saved in your own browser, on your own device, to make the site work. They are never sent to the developer.

  • No key. The site does not ask for or keep a NASA API key. A key saved by an earlier version of the Asteroid Tracker is deleted from your browser the next time you open it.
  • Where you were, such as the open tab, your filters and your scroll position, so the "Back" buttons return you to the same place. This is kept in your browser's session storage and is deleted when you close the tab.

Services your browser contacts

The pages load data and code straight from the services below. Like any website, each one receives the basic technical information every browser sends (your IP address, browser type and the address requested), under its own privacy policy.

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the site. GitHub logs each visitor's IP address for security (GitHub Pages data collection, GitHub Privacy Statement).
  • NASA and NASA/JPL services that supply the data and imagery: api.nasa.gov, GIBS, the NASA Image and Video Library, EONET and JPL's Solar System Dynamics (NASA Web Privacy Policy).
  • The US Geological Survey (earthquake.usgs.gov): the Image Explorer's globe reads the past week's earthquakes from it (USGS privacy policies).
  • ESA/Webb and ESA/Hubble (esawebb.org, esahubble.org and their picture servers cdn.esawebb.org and cdn.esahubble.org): the Image Explorer's Picture library and globe search their picture archives and show their pictures, under those sites' own privacy policies (ESA/Hubble privacy policy; ESA/Webb's is linked from its site).

Links to other sites

Some pages link to NASA, ESA (including its NEO Coordination Centre), the Minor Planet Center and other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Changes

If the site ever starts storing or sharing anything new, this page is updated first, with a new date at the top.

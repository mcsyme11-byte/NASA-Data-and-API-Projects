Words on this page

LD (lunar distance)
The average distance from Earth to the Moon, 384,400 km. 3 LD is three times farther than the Moon. Distances are measured from Earth's centre, so 7,500 km means about 1,100 km above the surface.
Potentially hazardous
NASA's flag for an object about 140 m or wider whose orbit comes within 7.5 million km of Earth's. It means "worth watching", not "on a collision course".
Size, est.
Worked out from brightness, so given as a range from the smallest to the largest likely diameter.

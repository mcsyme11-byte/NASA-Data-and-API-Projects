How to read this

Top view: the solar system seen from above. The Sun is in the middle; the blue ring is Earth's orbit (1 au, 150 million km). The brass line is the asteroid's orbit. Everything moves anticlockwise. Press Play or move the slider to watch Earth and the asteroid travel; the dashed line between them gives their distance.

Tilted and Side show that most asteroid orbits are slanted: the solid part of its orbit is above the plane of Earth's orbit, the dashed part below. It can only come really close to Earth near the two ✕ marks, where it crosses that plane. Near Earth zooms in around Earth.

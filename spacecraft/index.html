About this map

Every active spacecraft with a public position that this map follows: crewed stations, telescopes, missions to the Sun, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter and beyond, and satellites watching Earth. Military satellites appear only where their orbits are published; ordinary communication and navigation satellites (such as Starlink or GPS) are left out.

Positions of deep-space craft and those near the Lagrange points, the Moon and Mars come from NASA/JPL Horizons, copied twice a day; satellites in Earth orbit are worked out in your browser from CelesTrak's published orbital elements with the SGP4 model. Sizes are not to scale: each craft is drawn as an icon, while the Sun, planets and Moon are drawn at their true size once you zoom in far enough.

Mission facts checked November 12, 2026. A mission that ends is taken off the map.

Positions courtesy of NASA/JPL (Horizons) and CelesTrak; mission facts from NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO, CNSA and the other agencies, linked from each craft. Data courtesy of NASA. Not endorsed by any of these agencies.
